# Current portable extension

Original work by AfterCraft contributors, 2026. MIT OR Apache-2.0; both license texts accompany this package.

The extension implements public ScriptUI and After Effects keyframe scripting interfaces. Its
Bezier and motion mathematics, drawing, preset values and settings format are original work.
The general curve-editor workflow and physics-mode brief were informed by publicly documented
features of Flow and MFlow. Their implementation code, artwork, preset libraries and licensing
components are not included. No Adobe application implementation or assets are included.

After Effects and EffectCraft names identify compatible hosts; Current is an independent tool.
It does not need AfterCraft installed. The built-in native Current panel remains part of AfterCraft.
